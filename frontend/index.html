How does SachBite work?

What is SachBite?

SachBite is an online food ordering and delivery platform serving Kanti, Muzaffarpur, Bihar. It lets customers browse menus from local restaurants, place an order, and track the delivery live on a map.

What food can I order?

The menu shown on SachBite depends on what each listed restaurant currently offers — browse the Restaurants page to see available dishes near you.

How can I place an order?

Ordering on SachBite takes four steps:

  1. Browse restaurants and add items to your cart.
  2. Sign in with a one-time password (OTP) sent to your email or phone.
  3. Choose a payment method — UPI, card, or Cash on Delivery.
  4. Track your order live from the Track Order page once it's out for delivery.

What is the minimum order amount?

The minimum order value on SachBite is ₹99.

How much does delivery cost?

Delivery is ₹30 on orders below ₹300, and free above that.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are typically delivered by our delivery partner within 30–40 minutes, depending on your distance from the restaurant.
